Buyer questions

Is CSSBuys DE the official CSSBuy website?

No. CSSBuys DE is an independent informational site. It does not operate CSSBuy, take payments, provide warehouse services or control shipping routes.

What is a CSSBuy spreadsheet?

It is an independently organized list of product routes. A useful spreadsheet groups finds by category, link status, QC difficulty and shipping sensitivity instead of presenting an unexplained link dump.

What does W2C mean?

W2C means “where to cop.” In this guide it describes the route from a product name, image or spreadsheet entry to a live product listing that can be evaluated before purchase.

What should I check before using a W2C link?

Confirm that the page still opens, the seller listing matches the spreadsheet description, the selected variation is correct and the price has not changed.

What are QC photos?

QC photos are warehouse images used to review a received item before international shipment. They help reveal visible differences in color, shape, print, size markings, accessories and packaging.

Does a good-looking QC photo prove authenticity?

No. A warehouse photograph can help identify visible condition and listing mismatches, but it cannot by itself prove authenticity, material composition or long-term durability.

How should shoe QC be reviewed?

Compare both shoes side by side, inspect shape, stitching, sole alignment, size labels and visible glue marks, and decide whether the box is worth its shipping volume.

How should clothing QC be reviewed?

Check the selected size, front and back, print or embroidery placement, seams, tags and obvious stains. Ask whether the photos show every decision-critical area.
