Editorial & Product-Route Policy

This policy explains how workflow claims, independent recommendations and product-route data are kept separate.

Source hierarchy

Current public help-center, forwarding, contact and policy pages are used for platform-specific workflow statements. They establish what the platform publicly says, not independent proof of service performance. Third-party comments may suggest questions for research but are not treated as universal facts.

Fact versus editorial method

Every long guide includes an evidence-limit section. A public source may confirm that domestic tracking is entered for a forwarding order; the recommendation to keep a three-column evidence ledger is our editorial method. We do not present the second statement as an official rule.

Unconfirmed numbers

No universal processing time, QC-photo allowance, storage period, fee, exchange rate, route, customs result or delivery outcome is published unless a current source clearly supports it for the stated context. When a value can change by account, destination or parcel, readers are directed to the live screen.

Product routes

Before publication, a product card is checked for a working destination, exact visible title and matching first image. The source amount is converted to an approximate USD figure using the disclosed editorial rate. A route check does not verify stock, seller reliability, authenticity, hidden quality or future availability. Cards include the date of the check and category-specific fields a buyer can record.

Links and independence

Product and category calls to action use the linked product index selected for this site. Platform names may appear when necessary to explain the researched workflow, but this site does not provide promotional links to competing platforms. HooBuys.net is not the platform it discusses.

Updates and corrections

A page's modified date changes only after a meaningful content or data review. Thin or overlapping pages may be consolidated with a permanent redirect so visitors and search engines reach the more complete resource. When an error is found, the destination, claim and affected internal references are rechecked together.
