Privacy

Last updated August 13, 2026.

Information entered on this site

HooBuys.net is an informational site and does not ask visitors to create an account or enter order credentials. Product searches are submitted directly to the external product-index destination shown by the search form; do not enter private order information into that field.

Technical data

The hosting and security provider may process standard request information such as IP address, user agent, requested URL and security events to deliver and protect the site. Browser translation tools may set a language-preference cookie when selected.

External destinations

Links that leave HooBuys.net are governed by the destination's own privacy terms. Review the destination before providing personal, payment or order information.

Worksheet privacy

The downloadable CSV is stored and edited by you. Do not place passwords, card numbers, wallet credentials, identity documents or unnecessary personal addresses in it. Share only the rows required to explain a specific support issue.
