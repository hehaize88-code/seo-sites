Buyer FAQ

Short answers based on the evidence-led workflow used throughout the site.

Complete FAQ →
Is HooBuys.net the official Hoobuy website?

No. It is an independent guide and product-route hub. Confirm live service rules, fees, warehouse information and route options inside the service you use.

What evidence should I save for each order?

Keep the source URL, selected variant, order ID, domestic tracking number, package count, warehouse item ID and the date of each confirmed handoff.

Why can “delivered” and “stored” appear at different times?

A carrier delivery scan and warehouse intake, matching, QC and storage are separate events. Verify the tracking number and package count before escalating the missing handoff.

What can QC photos actually verify?

They can show visible identity, quantity, labels, measurements and obvious condition. They cannot prove authenticity, hidden construction, durability or fit.
