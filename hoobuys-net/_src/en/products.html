How these routes support an order-status record

A product card should not become the only evidence for an order. After opening the destination, save the live source URL and the exact option you selected. When a purchase status changes, compare the agent order with that record. When a warehouse item arrives, compare its visible identity and QC evidence with the same record. This keeps product discovery, order tracking and QC review connected without assuming a listing remains unchanged.
