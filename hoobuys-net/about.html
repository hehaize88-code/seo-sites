Who · How · Why

About HooBuys.net

An independent research and workflow site for buyers who need to understand the handoffs between product selection, purchasing, domestic delivery, forwarding, warehouse intake, QC, storage and parcel submission.

What this site does

HooBuys.net turns public workflow information into practical evidence records: which identifier to save, what an outside carrier scan proves, how a forwarding order differs from an agent purchase, and what a useful support request should contain. It also maintains a small set of current product-route examples so visitors can practise saving an exact source, title, image and selected variant.

What this site does not do

HooBuys.net is not Hoobuy, does not operate a warehouse, does not process payments or orders, and cannot see private account information. It does not authenticate products or guarantee sellers, QC results, fees, routes, customs outcomes, processing times or delivery dates.

How guides are produced

Platform-specific statements are checked against public help material and dated. We distinguish those statements from editorial methods such as the handoff ledger, mismatch packet and next-action framework. When a public source does not confirm a fixed number, price, period or outcome, the guide does not invent one. Product cards are checked against their destination page for the exact route, first image and displayed source amount; USD figures are clearly labelled estimates.

Why the site has a narrow focus

Several independent domains already cover generic Hoobuy spreadsheets, QC photos and shipping. HooBuys.net focuses on order status, forwarding and warehouse evidence so a visitor can solve a distinct operational question instead of reading another copy of the same broad guide.
