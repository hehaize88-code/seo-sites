QC Checklist for UK Buyers Before Shipping

A UK buyer QC checklist for shoes, clothing, accessories and warehouse photos before shipping.

Why QC matters

QC photos are the best chance to catch obvious problems before international shipping. After dispatch, changes become harder and more expensive.

A good QC process is not about perfection. It is about deciding whether the visible risk is acceptable.

Category-specific checks

Different product types require different review points. Trainers need side profile, heel balance, sizing and outsole checks. Hoodies and tops need measurements, print placement and colour consistency. Accessories need hardware, finish and included parts.

  • Confirm item type, colour and selected size.
  • Look for damage, stains or wrong options.
  • Ask for extra photos only when they answer a real question.
  • Approve only when the risk is acceptable.

How QC connects to shipping

QC choices shape the parcel. If one item looks risky, it may not be worth including in a UK-bound parcel with other approved items.

Turn the QC review into a recorded decision

Review identity first: item, colour, selected size, quantity and included accessories. Then inspect visible condition and any measurement that could change your decision. Keep the order reference attached to the images, especially when several similar products arrive together.

Use three outcomes in your own notes: accept, clarify or return requested. An unclear photograph is a reason to request the missing evidence; it is not a reason to assume the item passed. Ask for a specific measurement or angle, with readable endpoints, rather than simply requesting more images.

Resolve fit before packaging changes

The UK size guide explains why a label, foot length and insole measurement must not be treated as interchangeable. Compare the exact seller chart with a suitable reference. Keep tags and packaging intact while return eligibility is unresolved.

Hipobuy's public returns guidance ties routine warehouse requests to a limited window and seller conditions. Check the item promptly after storage rather than waiting for the rest of a haul. If a problem is confirmed, follow the returns and refunds process and keep that item out of the international parcel.
