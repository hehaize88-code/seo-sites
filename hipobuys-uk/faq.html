Is HipoBuys UK the official Hipobuy platform?

No. This is an independent information site. Orders, payments and parcel services are handled by the destination platform, not by this website.

What is a Hipobuy spreadsheet?

It is a product-discovery index. Check the original seller, selected option and current listing rather than treating a spreadsheet row as a guarantee.

What does W2C mean?

It means where to cop: a way to locate a product source. A useful W2C record includes the actual product link and enough details to identify the intended option.

Why does my product link not load?

Possible causes include an incomplete URL, a removed listing, a sign-in requirement or a restricted store. Read the visible message and compare the original marketplace page with the Hipobuy result.

Does a working link prove stock is available?

No. Recheck the current option, quantity and stock in the purchasing workflow.

Can a converter bypass an item restriction?

Do not assume it can. Use the official support process to resolve a classification question with accurate product details.

What should I check in QC photos?

Compare the received item with the ordered size, colour, quantity and visible condition. Ask for a clear view when an important detail cannot be assessed.

Do QC photos prove authenticity?

No. Photographs have limits and do not establish authenticity, hidden defects or long-term durability.

Are extra QC photos always free?

Check the current account service options. This website does not publish a universal free-photo allowance or fixed extra-photo price.

What belongs in the total cost?

Separate product price, domestic delivery, selected services, international freight, payment conversion and applicable destination charges. Subtract only a discount accepted at checkout.

Is a declaration service charge the same as UK import tax?

No. Keep a platform service charge separate from any destination tax or carrier handling charge, and check current official guidance for your shipment.

How do I estimate shipping?

Enter the actual destination, contents, weight and dimensions in the current estimator. Update the inputs after packing.

What is volumetric weight?

It is a weight measure calculated from parcel dimensions using the selected line’s rule. Check the live divisor and rounding instead of borrowing them from another carrier.

Which UK shipping line is best?

Compare eligible routes for your exact parcel using total cost, billed weight, tracking, protection and the displayed delivery range.

Does UK Line B always use the same carrier?

The name alone does not prove a permanent carrier mapping. Check the current route description and parcel tracking.

What does GB airlines mean on a parcel record?

Match the exact wording with the current account record and ask official support when unclear. It is not sufficient evidence to identify an airline or last-mile company.

What is rehearsal parcel used for?

Use the current service description and resulting packed measurements to improve a shipping comparison. Confirm available options and charges before requesting it.

Does removing boxes always save money?

No. The result depends on chargeable weight, minimum charges, service costs and the protection the product needs.

Can I add items after rehearsal?

Check the current workflow. A changed item list or packaging can make earlier measurements unsuitable for the final quote.

Does a coupon apply to every route?

Do not assume it does. Check validity, minimum spend, eligible services and the accepted checkout result.

Is a quiet tracking period proof that a parcel is lost?

No. Check the last recorded stage and current service guidance. Use the platform or identified carrier’s official support channel when an enquiry is appropriate.

When should I resolve a return or sizing issue?

Resolve it through the current order process before international dispatch and before discarding packaging that may be needed. Check the applicable seller and platform terms.

Should I put my address in a public support screenshot?

No. Crop or redact personal and payment details. Send private order evidence through the authenticated support channel.

Are the guides available in other languages?

Yes. Use the language links to open the equivalent article. All eight language editions contain the same guide catalogue.
