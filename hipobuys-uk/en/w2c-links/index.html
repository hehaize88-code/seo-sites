Before you continue

Keep the selected item, current order terms and the next decision together. Check the linked guides when you need measurements, payment records or a parcel plan.

Move from a product find to an exact order record

A discovery link should lead to the product you intended to inspect. Check the image, title and selected variant after opening it. Save the seller, source listing and the date, then note the colour, size and quantity. A product page with several options needs a record of the chosen option, not just its lowest displayed price.

Use a category or a specific model term when searching the current product finds. If a saved link changes, compare the new destination before treating it as a replacement. A visually similar product is not evidence that the seller, specifications or after-sales terms are identical.

Check the purchase before planning the parcel

Resolve sizing through the UK measurement guide, keep the initial payment separate from later freight, and identify anything that needs warehouse QC. Product discovery ends with a verifiable item record; the shipping decision comes after the actual contents and measurements are known.

Confirmed public facts

  • Hipobuy lists English, French, German, Italian, Polish, Portuguese and Spanish language options.
  • Hipobuy offers Custom Order, Forwarding Order, Shipping Estimate, FAQ and Beginner’s Guide entry points.
  • Hipobuy states that the shopping agent service is free.
  • The public cost formula is: item cost + international shipping fee + customs charges.
  • Item cost means product price plus domestic shipping from seller to Hipobuy warehouse.
  • International shipping is calculated by weight, shipping method and destination.
  • Customs declaration cost is generally RMB 10, depending on the shipping method.
  • The shipping estimate supports destination, weight, product type and parcel dimensions.
