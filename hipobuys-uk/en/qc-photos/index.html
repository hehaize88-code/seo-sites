Use Hipobuy warehouse QC photos to compare the item received with the product and option you ordered. Check quantity, colour, measurements, visible condition and included accessories before submitting an international parcel.

Start with the order record

Keep the source listing and selected option beside the photos. Compare size labels and visible dimensions with the seller's chart. A label alone does not establish fit, and a photograph without a readable ruler does not establish a measurement.

Ask for the missing evidence

If a seam, print, label or measurement is unclear, ask through the account for an appropriate additional view. Check any current service charge before requesting it. Public guidance does not establish a universal number of free photos or a fixed price for every extra view.

Understand the limits of photographs

Lighting and camera angle can affect colour and proportions. Photographs cannot establish authenticity, hidden defects, long-term durability or whether a garment will fit your body. Record uncertainty instead of converting a clear image into a quality guarantee.

Make the return decision before packing changes

Resolve missing pieces, the wrong option or visible damage through the current order process. Check the applicable return terms and timing. Keep necessary packaging until that decision is complete. Then use the rehearsal parcel checklist when packing measurements matter.

Preserve a useful record

Keep the dated listing, selected option, QC views and support outcome together. Share only cropped evidence when asking for advice; remove addresses, account details and payment information. Continue to the UK shipping-line guide after the item itself has passed your checks.
