QC Photos Guide for Hipobuy Orders

QC photos help buyers check visible details after items reach the warehouse. They do not guarantee every material detail, but they reduce avoidable mistakes.

Before you continue

Keep the selected item, current order terms and the next decision together. Check the linked guides when you need measurements, payment records or a parcel plan.

Request the photograph that resolves the uncertainty

Start with the order record and identify the unanswered question. A label photograph helps confirm the selected option; a ruler photograph may help compare dimensions; a close-up can show the location of visible damage. These requests serve different purposes. Check the current service availability and price before requesting additional work.

A useful measurement image shows the ruler's start and end, a readable unit and a consistent item position. If the ruler is diagonal or the garment is stretched, explain the issue and ask for a corrected measurement. More photographs do not improve the decision if they repeat the same ambiguity.

Keep image evidence within its limits

Photographs cannot establish every material property, hidden fault or authenticity claim. Distinguish what is visible from what a seller states. Use the UK inspection checklist for item-by-item decisions and the size guide for fit evidence before shipping.
