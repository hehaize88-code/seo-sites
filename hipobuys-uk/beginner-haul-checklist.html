Start smaller

A first parcel should teach the buyer how the workflow behaves. A smaller haul makes it easier to understand sizing, QC, packaging and shipping expectations.

Before ordering

Pick a category, compare a few product paths and save backups. Do not add random products just because they look interesting.

  • Choose one main category goal.
  • Check product options and sizing.
  • Read the QC guide before the warehouse stage.
  • Think about shipping volume early.

Before shipping

Review QC photos, item list, selected line and packaging choices. If one item is uncertain, hold it or request a targeted extra photo.

FAQ

How many items should a first haul include?

Fewer items are easier to manage.

Should I mix many categories?

Not at first. Keep the parcel coherent.
