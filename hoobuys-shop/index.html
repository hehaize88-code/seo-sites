Hoobuy finds and QC FAQ

How should I verify a W2C product route?

Open the source route, confirm the exact variant, colour and size, and keep that record beside the warehouse QC images.

What should I check first in QC photos?

Confirm item identity and quantity first, then review overall shape before inspecting category-specific details such as seams, soles or hardware.

When is another photo useful?

Request a precise view only when the current evidence hides an important area and the answer would change your shipping decision.

What should I confirm before parcel submission?

Review the items, available QC evidence, listed weight, route restrictions, packaging choices, address and final amount shown in the live account.

When can a video help?

A video may be useful for a moving or functional part, but first check whether that service is currently available and whether it would answer a specific question.

Why is there no fixed shipping price here?

Final cost can depend on destination, route, actual or volumetric weight, packaging and item attributes, so use the live checkout as the current source.
