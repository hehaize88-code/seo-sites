Product and QC basics

Does a product link verify an item?

No. Check the current seller listing, exact variant and your own warehouse evidence.

Where are my order photos?

Open the relevant order or warehouse item in your Hoobuy account and check its status and photos.

When is an extra photo useful?

When a specified angle or measurement can resolve a concrete question before shipping.

Is this calculator a shipping quote?

No. It illustrates volumetric weight; current route prices and conditions must be checked in your account.

Can I download the tracker?

Yes. The spreadsheet guide includes a blank CSV template for links, variants, QC decisions and costs.

Can I change language without losing the article?

Yes. The seven core guides have matching pages in all nine languages.
