Product and QC basics

Does a product link verify an item?

No. Check the current seller listing, exact variant and your own warehouse evidence.

Where are my order photos?

Open the relevant order or warehouse item in your Hoobuy account and check its status and photos.

When is an extra photo useful?

When a specified angle or measurement can resolve a concrete question before shipping.

Is this calculator a shipping quote?

No. It illustrates volumetric weight; current route prices and conditions must be checked in your account.

Can I download the tracker?

Yes. The spreadsheet guide includes a blank CSV template for links, variants, QC decisions and costs.

Can I change language without losing the article?

Yes. The ten core guides have complete matching pages in all nine languages; the older archive retains its original language and translation menu.

How long does Hoobuy shipping take?

Separate purchasing, seller delivery, warehouse inspection, packing and international transit. Use the current route estimate and your own confirmed events; this site does not promise a fixed delivery time.

Does Process Pending mean my parcel is in transit?

No. Check the item order, payment and purchase progress. International parcel tracking is a later and separate record.

Does label created mean the carrier has my parcel?

It records tracking information, not necessarily physical acceptance. Look for a carrier possession or movement event and check the account if the two records disagree.

Why does my Hoobuy link not work?

Possible observations include a copied address error, a loading or sign-in problem, an unavailable variant or a changed listing. Record the exact result and verify the original seller link.

Does an available product page mean every size is in stock?

No. Select the exact size, colour and quantity and check current availability and price before placing an order.

Can I replace a broken link with a similar product?

Treat it as a new candidate. Compare seller, listing identity, dimensions, variants, price and available evidence before transferring it to an order.

Does no public QC result mean the product failed inspection?

No. It means the service has no matching reference to show. Check your own warehouse order for private inspection evidence.

What inputs do I need for a Germany shipping quote?

Germany as the destination, the requested delivery area, actual contents, packed weight and outer dimensions. Record the quote date and packing assumptions.

Is the cheapest quoted route always suitable?

Only compare routes that accept the actual contents and dimensions. Check tracking, included services and delivery estimates as well as price.

Does free domestic shipping include delivery to my country?

Do not infer that. Seller-to-warehouse delivery and international parcel postage are separate parts of the purchase.

Why can the final shipping amount differ from an estimate?

Check final weight, box dimensions, billing increments and selected services against the saved estimate. Ask for clarification where the record does not explain the difference.

Will removing a shoe box always save money?

No. A change must affect the applicable chargeable weight or service price. Also consider protection and whether you want to retain the box.

Are tax and destination charges always included?

Check the selected service terms and current official destination guidance. This site does not assume inclusion from a route name or provide a guaranteed tax-free amount.

What should I send support about a delayed parcel?

Use the private support channel with the parcel identifier, route, last confirmed event, date and a precise question. Keep addresses and payment information out of public posts.
