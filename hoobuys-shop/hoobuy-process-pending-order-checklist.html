Hoobuy Process Pending: What to Check Before Reordering

“Process Pending” is a status to investigate, not a reliable delivery countdown. Identify the last confirmed step before deciding whether to wait, ask support or request cancellation.

Save the current order record

Record the order number, payment time, selected variant, amount and the exact status text. Take a dated screenshot of the order and check for messages requesting confirmation, a variant change or an additional payment. Do not post account details or payment identifiers in a public discussion.

Compare the order record with your payment history. A bank authorisation, wallet balance and a completed order payment are different records. If they disagree, ask support to reconcile the transaction before paying again.

Separate purchase progress from delivery progress

Check whether there is evidence that the agent has purchased the item, whether the seller has dispatched it, and whether a domestic tracking number exists. A seller-to-warehouse parcel and an international parcel are different stages with different tracking records.

Do not infer warehouse arrival from payment alone. QC photos generally become relevant after receipt and inspection; an empty photo section is not enough to diagnose a purchasing delay. Ask which step is pending rather than asking only where the parcel is.

Send a support message that can be answered

Use a short, factual request: “Order [number], paid [date and time], currently shows [exact status]. Please confirm whether purchase is complete, whether this variant is available, and whether I need to act. If purchase cannot proceed, what cancellation or refund options currently apply?”

Attach the order screenshot and relevant payment evidence through the account's support channel. Keep one conversation for the same issue when possible. A clear timeline is more useful than several conflicting messages or an unsupported accusation.

Decide whether waiting still fits your plan

Compare the response with your actual deadline, seller availability and the rest of your parcel. Avoid treating another shopper's waiting time as a promise for your order. This guide does not claim a fixed processing time or a guaranteed cancellation window.

Before placing a replacement order, confirm what will happen to the first one. Ask whether cancellation is still possible and where any approved refund will go. A refund to an account balance is not automatically a completed withdrawal to your original payment method.

Close the loop when the status changes

Save the support response and check that the order itself reflects the agreed next step. If a substitute size or colour is proposed, compare it with the original requirement before accepting. Update your product tracker so a shared haul does not contain duplicate or unwanted items.

Once warehouse photos are available, inspect the actual item before combining it into a parcel. If there is still no clear answer, follow up with the same order number and timeline. Use current account terms for deadlines, fees and eligibility; do not rely on an old forum post.
