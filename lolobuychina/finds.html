Make each find useful before adding it to a haul

Match the product

Save the original source ID, selected colour, size and variant. Similar promotional images do not establish that two listings offer the same item.

Check fit

Compare the listing's measurement method with a garment or shoe you already use. A size label alone cannot establish fit.

Review the evidence

Keep listing pictures separate from photographs of a received item. Describe any visible uncertainty and request a focused check when that service is available.

Budget the parcel

Separate the item price from domestic delivery, optional services, currency conversion and the eventual international parcel quote.

Choose your next research step

Read the shoe sizing and QC guide for footwear, or the hoodie measurement guide for clothing. Use the link and product-ID tool when a saved source needs checking.

For more choices, use the category directory. For parcel planning, start with the shipping guide. Keep uncertain stock, measurements and charges marked as unknown until you have current evidence.
