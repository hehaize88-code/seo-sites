LoloBuy QC Photos & Inspection Checklist

Start with the photos assigned to your own warehouse item. Public QC references can help you decide which details to inspect, but they cannot confirm the condition of a different order.

Find the right QC record

  1. Open the item in your current LoloBuy order or warehouse record.
  2. Match the order reference, source item ID, selected colour, size and quantity.
  3. Review all available photographs and their inspection context.
  4. Request a focused image or measurement when the current service offers it.

If you only have a spreadsheet reference, use the product-link finder to identify a catalogue candidate. It is not a search engine for private warehouse photos.

Check the details that affect your decision

ItemEvidence to reviewNext action
ShoesPair symmetry, size labels, sole edges, accessories and requested measurements.Compare the seller's size chart; do not infer US or UK sizing from one number.
Hoodies and clothingFlat chest width, length, sleeves, labels, seams and print placement.Compare with a garment that fits, using the same measurement method.
AccessoriesHardware, closures, quantity, missing parts and packaging.Ask about missing evidence before accepting the item.

Record a clear decision before shipping

Mark each item accepted, needs clarification, or return requested. Photos do not prove authenticity, hidden performance or long-term durability. Colour can vary with lighting. Keep unresolved items out of the parcel while you clarify the available next action.
