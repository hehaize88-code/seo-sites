Independent cost-audit guide

A trustworthy LoloBuy fee check does not begin with a percentage copied from a forum or an old guide. It begins with one current order, one selected currency, one payment method and the final amount shown before confirmation. Separate every cost line, calculate the effective difference and keep the result dated. If the screen does not identify a charge, label it unexplained rather than inventing a permanent service-fee rule.

Verified platform context: LoloBuy's public website was reachable on the fact-check date and continues to describe a product-link ordering flow followed by warehouse inspection and photographs. The public homepage does not provide one universal, complete fee table that can replace the live order and payment screens. No fixed service percentage, payment surcharge, exchange-rate markup, discount or refund deduction is claimed in this guide.

Separate the fee question into cost layers

“What are the LoloBuy fees?” is too broad to answer responsibly with one number. A reverse-shopping-agent order can contain the seller's product price, seller-to-warehouse delivery, a purchasing or service line, optional warehouse services, payment processing, currency conversion, later international freight and destination-side costs. Not every order shows every layer, and the same label may appear at a different step.

Begin with the transaction you are auditing. The first product payment and the later international parcel payment solve different problems. Do not divide a parcel fee by the product amount and call the result a purchasing service rate. Likewise, do not treat Chinese domestic delivery as a platform fee merely because it appears in the same total.

Capture a same-moment payment snapshot

A fee comparison fails when its inputs come from different times. Seller prices, exchange rates, discounts and payment options can move. Take the product-order subtotal and the final payable amount from the same checkout session, after the exact variant and quantity are selected. Record the date, time, account currency and payment method. If you change the method, save a second complete snapshot.

The snapshot should show the amount before payment without exposing private data. Keep product reference, item subtotal, domestic delivery, named service lines, discount, platform conversion if displayed, payment charge and final amount. Redact card digits, account balance, address and identity information. A screenshot without the currency or total cannot support a reliable calculation.

If the page updates after a payment method is selected, use the updated total. Do not compare the starting basket in one currency with a bank notification in another and attribute the entire difference to LoloBuy. Your bank or card provider can apply its own conversion or cross-border charge after the platform has shown its amount.

Calculate an effective difference without mislabelling it

First create an explained subtotal by adding the seller price, displayed domestic delivery and every named service or option. Then subtract any accepted discount. Compare that result with the platform's final payable amount in the same currency. The difference is an audit signal. It is not automatically a service fee because it may include rounding, payment processing or conversion.

For example, suppose a hypothetical order shows a 420-unit item subtotal, 18 units of domestic delivery and a named 6-unit optional service. The explained subtotal is 444 units. If the payable amount is 451 units, the unexplained difference is 7 units. The effective difference is 7 divided by 444, or about 1.58%. Those figures are a teaching example only; they are not current LoloBuy prices or rates.

Audit currency conversion as a separate decision

Identify the seller or order currency, the currency selected in the account, the platform amount displayed before confirmation and the currency your payment provider will actually debit. A public market rate is a reference, not the price a platform or bank must use. Timing, spread, processor rules and card terms can all produce a difference.

If the checkout lets you compare currencies without confirming payment, record each quote at nearly the same moment and use the same underlying order. Calculate the implied rate by dividing the payable amount by the source-currency total. Then compare payment methods on the final debit currency and total—not on a headline promise such as “no fee.” A method with no separate fee can still use a different conversion result.

Your bank statement belongs to another evidence layer. After payment, compare the platform confirmation with the amount actually debited. If they differ, check whether the bank identifies a foreign-transaction or conversion charge. Do not assign a provider charge to the shopping platform without evidence.

Compare payment methods on the same order

Hold the basket constant. Use the same product, quantity, domestic delivery, optional services, discount state and currency. Switch only the payment method, when the live interface permits a nonbinding comparison. Record availability, displayed fee, payable total, refund destination and any material term shown before confirmation. Do not assume that a method available to another country or account is available to yours.

A simple decision table needs five fields: method, debit currency, named processing charge, final payable amount and evidence timestamp. Add a note for provider-side charges that are not visible in the platform. If a method cannot be selected safely without initiating payment, do not manufacture a comparison. Report only the options you could observe.

  • Use one unchanged order as the baseline.
  • Record the currency at every step.
  • Separate named charges from calculated differences.
  • Keep discounts visible as reductions, not hidden inside a net rate.
  • Do not generalize one account, country or date to every buyer.

Keep optional services out of the payment-fee bucket

A warehouse service can be worth buying and still not be a payment fee. Record the exact service name, which item or parcel it applies to, when it is charged and whether the price is shown before confirmation. Examples might concern inspection, photos, measurements or packing, but this guide does not claim that any specific option is always available or priced at a fixed amount.

The same rule applies to international parcel add-ons. A packing or protection choice belongs to parcel planning, not to the purchasing-service rate. If a total changes after an option is added, preserve the before-and-after screens. This establishes the marginal cost of that selected option for that transaction without turning it into a permanent platform tariff.

Handle coupons and account credits transparently

A discount lowers the amount paid; it does not prove the underlying fee disappeared. Record the subtotal before the coupon, the accepted reduction and the total after it. Note the date and any eligibility text visible at checkout. Do not quote an advertised coupon value if the current order does not accept it, and do not subtract an expired promotion when comparing methods.

Account balance, promotional credit and cash payment are not necessarily equivalent. A balance may come from a prior refund or top-up, while a promotion may have restrictions. Keep source and value separate. If the checkout does not explain how a credit affects a future refund, do not promise an outcome.

Final fee-audit checklist

  1. Confirm the exact product variant, quantity and seller price.
  2. Record China domestic delivery as its own line.
  3. List every named platform or optional service.
  4. Apply discounts as separate reductions.
  5. Save account currency, debit currency and payment method.
  6. Compare the explained subtotal with the final payable amount.
  7. Label unexplained differences without guessing their cause.
  8. Check provider-side charges after payment separately.
  9. Date every claim and limit it to the observed transaction.
  10. Recheck the current screen before relying on an older guide.

The purpose of a LoloBuy service and payment fee audit is not to force every cost into one percentage. It is to make the transaction explainable. When each layer has a name, currency, evidence source and date, buyers can compare choices without turning a temporary checkout result into a platform-wide promise.
