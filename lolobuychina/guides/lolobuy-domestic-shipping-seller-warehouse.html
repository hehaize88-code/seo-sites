Independent shipping-stage guide

LoloBuy domestic shipping is the China-side movement from a marketplace seller to the purchasing warehouse. It happens after the product order is accepted and before warehouse QC, consolidation or international parcel submission. Track it as a separate cost and status chain: seller handling, courier dispatch, delivery scan and warehouse intake.

Know which shipping stage you are looking at

A reverse-shopping-agent order has at least two transport stages. First, the Chinese seller sends the item to the agent's warehouse. Later, accepted warehouse items are prepared for international delivery. Those stages involve different addresses, carriers, evidence and costs. A seller dispatch scan is not international dispatch, and a domestic tracking number is not necessarily the number that will follow the final parcel to your destination.

Keep four records separate: the seller's order or purchase status; the China domestic courier record; the warehouse receipt and QC record; and the later international parcel record. When a page says “shipped,” ask which actor shipped what to whom. That one question prevents most status confusion.

The stage is complete only when the correct item is associated with your warehouse account and ready for the relevant intake or QC review. A courier “delivered” event can be useful evidence, but it does not by itself prove that the warehouse has identified, processed and photographed the item.

Separate domestic freight from product and international cost

Record the product amount and seller-to-warehouse delivery as different fields. A listing may show free domestic delivery, a fixed amount, a destination-dependent charge or no usable figure until the order is created. Zero should mean the live order explicitly shows no charge; blank should mean you have not confirmed it. Do not copy the cheapest option's freight to another size, seller or listing.

When several items come from one seller, check whether the current order treats them as separate shipments or a combined seller dispatch. Do not assume one domestic charge will cover every item. A seller can split stock, dispatch from different locations or send an incomplete quantity. Preserve the order-level breakdown rather than dividing a shared amount into invented item prices.

The domestic cost belongs in a total landed-cost worksheet, but it should not be confused with international freight. The later parcel quote depends on accepted items, packed weight and dimensions, available routes and current terms. Paying China-side delivery does not establish the final shipping cost.

Read the status chain as a sequence of responsibilities

Order submitted or purchasing

At this point the product link and selected option should be unambiguous. Save the source URL, seller name where visible, color, size, version, quantity and the amounts displayed. If purchasing is waiting on clarification, domestic shipping has not meaningfully begun. Resolve option questions before treating the seller as late.

Seller processing or awaiting dispatch

This normally means the purchasing stage exists but no useful carrier movement has been recorded. Stock confirmation, order handling or a seller question may still be open. The official public page does not provide one guaranteed seller dispatch time, so compare the current status, any seller note and the order's displayed dates instead of applying a borrowed deadline.

Domestic dispatched or in transit

Record the China courier name when shown, domestic tracking identifier, first acceptance scan and latest event. A number without a carrier acceptance event may be newly created, entered incorrectly or waiting for pickup. Translate the event, not just the status label, and keep the original text if the wording is uncertain.

Delivered to warehouse

Save the delivery date, time, city or facility clue and any recipient marker that appears in the domestic record. Then wait for the warehouse-side item to be matched and processed. A busy intake period can create a gap between courier delivery and account visibility. That gap is a warehouse handoff question, not an international-carrier delay.

Warehouse received and QC available

Confirm that the warehouse entry belongs to the correct order and quantity. LoloBuy publicly describes warehouse inspection and photographs, but the public page does not define a universal photo set or processing time. Review the evidence shown for the actual item. Domestic shipping is now historical evidence; the next decision is acceptance, a focused question or the current order action.

Diagnose a domestic-shipping delay from the last reliable event

Start with the last event, not the total number of days. If there is no seller dispatch, the seller or purchasing handoff is the open stage. If a tracking number exists without acceptance, the seller-to-courier handoff is unclear. If the parcel has moving courier scans, the domestic carrier owns the current transport stage. If it shows delivered but no warehouse item appears, focus on matching and intake.

Use elapsed time only in context. Compare the order's own dates, stated seller conditions and support options. Weekends, public events, stock arrangements and local courier exceptions can affect movement, but do not invent one of those explanations for a specific order without evidence. A quiet tracking page tells you that no new public event is visible; it does not prove loss, seller fraud or warehouse error.

  • No purchase confirmation: verify that the source and option are complete.
  • No dispatch: save the order status and any seller handling note.
  • Number, no carrier scan: preserve when the number first appeared.
  • In transit, no movement: record the exact last location and event.
  • Delivered, no intake: compare tracking, order identity and warehouse-receipt status.
  • Partial arrival: match quantity and each domestic number before escalating.

Build a support-ready domestic shipping evidence pack

A useful request is short because the supporting record is complete. Include the order reference, exact selected variant, seller name if visible, domestic carrier and number, latest event with date, and the specific missing handoff. Attach the full tracking view and order status when the interface permits. A cropped “delivered” word without the number or timeline is weaker evidence.

Write an observable request: “Domestic tracking shows delivery to the warehouse area on 13 August, but the order has no warehouse-received entry; please check whether this tracking number has been matched.” That is more actionable than “package missing.” Do not demand an outcome the evidence does not establish.

Remove full addresses, payment details, account credentials and unrelated parcel numbers before sharing. Preserve originals privately. If a seller sent several parcels, list each identifier and quantity on its own line. If the platform shows a support action or deadline for the current order, use that live route promptly; this guide does not promise a refund, replacement or claim result.

Close the China-side record when the warehouse takes over

After intake, compare product identity, color, size or version, quantity and visible condition with the saved order. Use the QC photo checklist for that decision. A completed domestic shipment proves that something reached the warehouse process; it does not prove that the item matches, works, fits, is authentic or can use every international route.

QC photo checklist

When several accepted items are ready, consolidation becomes a new decision. Compare compatibility, dimensions, protection and current route eligibility before assuming one parcel is cheapest. The domestic freight already paid remains an item-stage cost; it should not be merged into the international quote or forgotten when calculating landed cost.

Seller-to-warehouse checklist

  1. Save the original product link and exact selected option.
  2. Record the displayed product amount and China domestic freight separately.
  3. Identify who currently owns the handoff: buyer, seller, courier or warehouse.
  4. Save the first scan, latest event and any delivery event with dates.
  5. Match every tracking number to its order and quantity.
  6. Do not treat courier delivery as completed warehouse intake.
  7. Review identity and QC evidence once the item appears in the warehouse.
  8. Carry domestic freight into the landed-cost worksheet as its own layer.

The practical goal is not to predict a perfect timeline. It is to know where the item is supposed to be, what evidence supports the last handoff and which party can answer the next question. That turns “LoloBuy domestic shipping” from one vague status into a traceable seller-to-warehouse record.
