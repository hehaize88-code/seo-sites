LoloBuy FAQ

Practical answers for product research, inspection and parcel planning. Use your current account and order terms for transaction-specific conditions.

What is LoloBuyChina?

An independent product discovery and buying guide. It does not operate LoloBuy or process your orders.

What does W2C mean?

W2C means “where to cop”: the source product link or listing used to identify an item.

What does QC mean?

QC means quality check. In this guide, it refers to reviewing warehouse photos and available measurements before shipping.

Is a spreadsheet listing proof of stock?

No. Verify the current seller listing, selected variant and checkout. A saved card can outlive a product or price.

Where do product cards open?

Cards open their matching source catalogue records. Check the product reference and selected option on that page.

Why does an ID search show several items?

An unknown identifier opens catalogue search. Results are candidates; compare the source ID and product details before choosing.

Can I use Taobao, Weidian or 1688 links?

The finder recognises common product-link formats and checks established mappings. An unknown link opens search; platform import availability must be checked separately.

Why does a LoloBuy link fail?

Possible causes include an incomplete address, a source listing change, unavailable options or an import problem. Check the original source before retrying.

Where are my warehouse QC photos?

Look in the relevant item record in your current LoloBuy account. Match the order reference and selected variant before reviewing the images.

Can public QC photos prove my item is correct?

No. They describe another reference or inspection. Review the warehouse item assigned to your own order.

Do QC photos prove authenticity?

No. Photos can show visible details and requested measurements, but do not establish authenticity, hidden performance or long-term durability.

Does LoloBuy use US or UK shoe sizes?

The seller listing determines the size system. Check its chart and measurements; a size number alone does not identify US, UK or EU sizing.

How should I compare hoodie sizes?

Compare flat garment measurements with a hoodie that fits. Use the same chest-width, length and sleeve measurement method, not only S, M or L labels.

Is international shipping included in the product price?

Do not assume so. Record the item, domestic delivery, selected services and international parcel quote separately.

Why can the final shipping cost change?

Final packing, weight, dimensions, eligible routes and selected services can differ from an earlier estimate. Compare the same inputs and currency.

Is volumetric weight always charged?

Use the rule shown for the selected route. Some services use actual weight, others volume or the higher value; the divisor is not universal.

Does consolidation always save money?

No. Compare the complete quotes. Fewer parcels can reduce repeated base charges, but larger dimensions can increase billable weight.

Can every item travel on one route?

No. Check destination, item properties and current route restrictions, especially for batteries, liquids, fragile or bulky goods.

Does removing a shoe box always reduce cost?

No. The controlling weight or billing increment may stay the same. Compare the actual quote and the protection lost before removing packaging.

Does free storage extend a return deadline?

No. Storage and seller return conditions are separate. Check the deadline and eligibility shown for the exact order.

Can I cancel a paid order?

Check its current purchasing stage and available action. Obtain confirmation that the order is stopped; a message alone is not a completed cancellation.

Are all purchases returnable?

No. Eligibility depends on the item, seller, purchase channel and current terms. Manual or special purchases can have different conditions.

Is a balance credit the same as a bank refund?

No. Record the approved amount and destination. A platform balance credit and a refund to the original payment method are different outcomes.

Why does tracking say delivered before I receive the parcel?

Check whether the number belongs to seller-to-warehouse delivery or the international parcel. Domestic delivery to the warehouse is not delivery to your home.

Are coupon codes and reference prices guaranteed?

No. Check the live eligible transaction, expiry, currency and final payable amount. This site does not publish an unverified working coupon code.
