LoloBuy Shipping Cost & Parcel Guide

Plan Lolobuy shipping by checking destination, actual and volumetric weight, route restrictions, packaging and the final checkout quote.

How to use this page

Shipping is not a fixed product price. Consolidation, parcel dimensions, destination and route eligibility can all change the final cost, so use the current platform quote before paying.

Check actual and volumetric weight

Use warehouse and packed-parcel data when available. Large boxes and bulky packaging can raise billable volume even when scale weight is moderate. Compare the parcel dimensions as well as the weight before selecting a route.

Confirm route eligibility

Available routes depend on the destination and item types. Batteries, liquids, fragile goods and other restricted categories can narrow the choices. Review the live route conditions for the exact parcel instead of relying on an older screenshot or another buyer’s quote.

Plan compatible consolidation

Group accepted items by protection and route compatibility, not only by arrival date. Consolidation may reduce repeated base charges, but a larger parcel is not automatically cheaper when dimensions increase volumetric weight.

Review packaging and checkout

Protective packaging can help fragile or shape-sensitive items, but it may add weight or volume. Confirm recipient details, packaging choices, the final live quote and current terms before submitting the parcel.

Build a complete shipping budget

Before warehouse arrival

Record product price, China-side delivery and the conversion shown at payment. Keep optional service charges separate from international freight.

After packing

Record the packed weight, outside dimensions, selected route, packaging and final quote. Use the route's own billing rules; a comparison estimate is not the payable total.

Worked example: volume can change the comparison

In a hypothetical route using a divisor of 6,000, a 40 × 30 × 25 cm box has a volumetric weight of 5 kg. If its actual weight is 3 kg and that route bills the higher value, the calculation starts from 5 kg before its own rounding and other rules. This divisor and rule are illustrative, not a published universal LoloBuy tariff.
