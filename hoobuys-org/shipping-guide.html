Parcel checklist

  1. Confirm every item and selected variant against its saved source record.
  2. Resolve unclear QC evidence before consolidation.
  3. Use packed weight and measured dimensions, not only product-page estimates.
  4. Check current route restrictions for every item type in the parcel.
  5. Compare protection, box-retention and insurance terms before payment.
  6. Confirm the final amount and delivery details on the live checkout page.

Prepare a parcel record

  1. List the warehouse identifiers for the items you have approved.
  2. Record the destination and the actual contents.
  3. Keep item weights separate from the finished parcel weight.
  4. Record length, width and height after the proposed packing is known.
  5. Compare eligible routes and preserve the date and currency of each estimate.

Keep packing choices explicit

Write down whether original boxes, protective material or other packaging are required. A smaller parcel is useful only if the configuration is feasible and still protects the contents you intend to receive. Recheck dimensions when the configuration changes.

Follow the detailed guides

Use the shipping calculator and weight guide for units, volumetric calculations and a worked example. Use the total-cost budget to keep domestic delivery, international shipping and optional charges on separate lines.

Complete the QC photo review before adding an unresolved item to a parcel. Keep any question about current order options separate from a shipping estimate.
