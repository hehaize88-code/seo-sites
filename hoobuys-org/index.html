Quick answers

What should a W2C record contain?

Record the marketplace, seller, exact listing, selected variant, date checked and the evidence you still need before ordering.

What should I check first in QC photos?

Confirm identity first: product type, colour, size and quantity. Then review measurements, condition, construction and included pieces.

Which spreadsheet fields are most useful?

Use product name, category, source URL, selected variant, link status, QC status, follow-up request, packed weight, route notes and final decision.

When should I recheck a saved link?

Recheck before ordering and again when a listing title, seller, price or variant set changes. A spreadsheet row is a dated research record, not a permanent guarantee.

Why are fixed shipping prices not listed?

Destination, route, actual weight, volumetric weight, packaging and item restrictions can all change the final amount.

Where should I confirm current fees and coupons?

Use the live account, service panel and checkout page because exchange rates, service options and promotion rules can change.
