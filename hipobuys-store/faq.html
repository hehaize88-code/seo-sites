Hipobuy FAQ: Orders, QC, Shipping and Returns

Answers to 20 practical Hipobuy questions: product links, warehouse status, photo checks, shipping estimates, return requests and wallet credit.

Is HipoBuys Store the official Hipobuy service?

No. This is an independent guide and product-link directory. Orders, payments and support belong to the service used for purchase.

Does clicking a product link place an order?

No. Review the destination listing, selected option and checkout before making any purchase.

Can an old spreadsheet prove current stock?

No. Recheck the actual listing and variant; saved links and examples may change.

Where do I get a forwarding address?

Use the current forwarding instructions in your official account, including the receiving reference. A company office address is not a substitute.

Does delivered mean the warehouse has finished QC?

Not necessarily. Carrier delivery, intake, inspection and storage are separate events.

How long does Hipobuy take to deliver?

Add the relevant seller, domestic, warehouse and international stages. Use the selected line's current estimate rather than a universal deadline.

Does a tracking number prove carrier collection?

No. Check the carrier's acceptance event and the parcel status.

Which tracking number should I use?

Use domestic tracking for seller-to-warehouse delivery and parcel or final-mile tracking for the international journey.

Why are public QC photos missing?

The particular service may have no matching reference. Check the exact listing; absence alone does not prove a quality problem.

Do another buyer's photos replace my QC?

No. Confirm the images for your own received item and selected variant.

What should an extra-photo request include?

Order identifier, exact area, angle, measurement method if needed, and a request to confirm any fee.

Can QC photos prove authenticity or comfort?

No. They show visible features and measurements, not every material property or how a product will feel.

Are all size charts interchangeable?

No. Match the seller, product, chart type and units; body measurements and flat garment widths differ.

What is volumetric weight?

A dimension-based calculation using length × width × height divided by the line's divisor. Billing and rounding depend on that line.

Is this shipping calculator a live quote?

No. It illustrates weight arithmetic. Current rates, route eligibility and extras come from the official account.

Why did my final shipping cost change?

Compare final packed weight, dimensions, route, included services and accepted discounts with the earlier estimate.

Does splitting a parcel always save money?

No. Add both quotes and extra charges, including any repeated starting cost.

Does a photo request pause the return period?

Do not assume so. Check the actual deadline and submit the relevant return request promptly if needed.

Is an approved return already money on my card?

No. Track return completion, wallet credit and any separate withdrawal as distinct steps.

Where should I send order or refund questions?

Use official support with the correct order or parcel reference. Do not publish passwords, verification codes or payment details.
