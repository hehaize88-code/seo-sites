Hipobuy Returns & Refunds: Request, Fees and Wallet Credit

Check Hipobuy return eligibility, the warehouse request window, return costs and wallet credit. Track each step before expecting money back to your card.

For a Hipobuy return, first check the item's warehouse status, the seller's terms and whether the packaging remains eligible. A request, seller approval, completed return, wallet credit and withdrawal are separate milestones. This guide explains what evidence to keep at each point; it does not promise approval or a universal refund-processing time.

Is your problem a cancellation, return or delivery claim?

Start with where the item actually is. If purchasing has not been completed, ask about cancellation on the order. If the seller has dispatched it, there may already be a physical shipment to resolve. If it is stored at the warehouse, the seller-return procedure is the relevant starting point. If the parcel has left internationally, report the parcel issue through the appropriate after-sales channel instead of assuming a warehouse return can still happen.

Write down the order identifier and, where applicable, the parcel identifier. A support request containing only a bank top-up amount can be difficult to match to one item in a larger purchase. Keep the product payment, account recharge and international shipping payment separate in your notes. Returning one item does not automatically cancel every item bought with the same account balance.

Check the request window against the actual warehouse timestamp

The official Chinese returns notice reviewed on 6 November 2026 describes a five-day request period for eligible warehoused goods, calculated as 120 hours from the next whole hour after warehousing. Seller participation and resale condition also apply. Its fee section lists both domestic shipping legs plus a CNY 5 service charge for a buyer-reason return, including possible repayment of original delivery advertised as free. Check the current order before relying on these terms.

Record the timestamp exactly as shown, including the account's time zone. Do not start a personal countdown from the day you opened a spreadsheet, the seller's dispatch or an international tracking event. Ask support which timestamp controls if the displayed labels are ambiguous. Submit a clear request promptly; do not assume that asking for another photo pauses the deadline. The application window is not a promise that the whole return will finish in five days.

Preserve the condition that the seller needs

Before instructing the warehouse to remove tags, seals, accessories or retail packaging, decide whether you still need to assess a return. Altering the item can remove evidence or affect eligibility. A request to reduce parcel volume should come after the inspection decision, particularly when the packaging is part of the product you expected to receive.

Keep the saved seller listing, selected size and colour, order confirmation and original QC images together. If a photo is unclear, ask for one precise view without requesting unnecessary alteration. For example, request a readable size label while leaving the tag attached. Personalized and category-specific products require particular care: a generic return headline cannot establish the conditions for the item in your own order.

Explain the reason with a comparison the seller can check

Separate a mismatch from a change of preference. If you ordered a blue size M and the received label shows black size S, state both observations and attach the matching order record. If the item matches your chosen option but you no longer want it, describe that accurately. Do not label a change of mind as a defect just to obtain different treatment.

For visible damage, identify the location and the image that shows it. Retain the original photo rather than replacing it with an edited image that obscures the surrounding item. A colour difference visible only under unusual lighting may need a neutral-light retake. Ask for an explanation of the proposed return charges and who is responsible, keeping the answer with the request. Evidence makes the case assessable; it cannot guarantee the seller's decision.

Compare return costs with the money you can recover

Use the amount actually paid for that item as the starting point. Then list every proposed deduction and distinguish a seller-return charge from an international parcel charge. If a coupon or combined order affected the payment, ask which amount is allocated to the item being returned. Do not assume that the original listing price is the amount that will be credited.

For an illustration only, suppose an item payment is CNY 160 and a confirmed return proposal lists CNY 12 outbound delivery, CNY 10 return delivery and CNY 5 handling. The comparison is 160 minus 27, or CNY 133, before any other explicitly agreed adjustment. These are invented accounting inputs, not a Hipobuy quotation. Their purpose is to show why the gross item price and net recovery are different. Confirm deductions before approving the proposal.

Follow the item and the refund as two separate records

Maintain a short timeline: request submitted, seller response, return dispatched, return received, refund completed and account credit recorded. Some steps may not be individually visible in the interface. In that case, ask which step is pending rather than repeatedly asking whether everything is done. A return request acknowledgement is not evidence that money has already been credited.

Hipobuy's official status notice distinguishes a return under review from a completed return and says completed returns and cancelled orders are credited to the account balance. Look for the corresponding balance transaction and amount. Save its date and reference privately. If you cannot match the credit, provide the order identifier, completion status and amount expected to official support. Avoid publishing your wallet history or personal payment details in a public comment.

Wallet credit is not the same as a card withdrawal

A balance entry shows that value has reached the platform account. Sending eligible funds back through a payment channel is a separate process with its own available methods and terms. Check the current wallet interface and ask about the specific transaction before assuming that the original card will be refunded automatically. A purchase refund, recharge reversal and withdrawal request should not be merged into one label.

If a withdrawal is available and you choose to request it, record its reference, destination method, requested amount and status separately from the product return. Once support confirms that a transfer has been released, any payment-provider reference may help trace it. This guide cannot establish a processing deadline from a balance screenshot. Use the timeframe supplied for that particular transaction, and follow up when that stated timeframe has passed.

Use one concise follow-up message

A useful message says: “Order [identifier] was marked [status] at [time]. I requested [cancellation/return] on [date]. The latest response says [pending step]. The amount shown is [amount and currency]. Please confirm the next required action, any deduction, and whether the refund is still pending or has reached the wallet.” Replace the brackets with your own facts inside the official support channel.

Keep one thread where possible so a later reply can be interpreted against the earlier decision. Attach only the evidence needed for the issue, and do not share passwords or verification codes. If the item has already entered a parcel, mention that immediately and ask whether any hold is still possible. Finish by checking the actual outcome: item status, correct credit and any separate withdrawal. That provides a more reliable answer than counting elapsed days from the original purchase.
