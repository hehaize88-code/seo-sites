Product-find FAQ

Complete FAQ

How are product routes checked?

The current title, first image, price reference and destination URL are matched and dated. Always verify the selected option again.

What should I check first in QC photos?

Begin with product identity, colour, size and quantity. Then review measurements, construction, included pieces and visible damage.

Why can shipping estimates change?

Measured weight, dimensions, category, packaging, destination, route eligibility and selected services all matter.

What does W2C mean here?

It means finding the current product route and recording enough evidence to decide whether the listing is still relevant.
