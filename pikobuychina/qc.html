PikoBuy QC Photos: Check the Variant Before Shipping

Start with the order specification

Compare warehouse photographs with the seller, exact variant and quantity saved at purchase. Inspect both the overall item and details that affect your decision. A missing view is an open question, not evidence that everything is correct.

Ask for a precise photo or measurement when it would change your decision, and check its current fee. Photos help with visible details but do not establish authenticity, composition, durability or internal performance. Review current return eligibility promptly before international dispatch.
