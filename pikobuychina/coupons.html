PikoBuy Coupons Guide

Evaluate shipping coupons, referral rewards and campaign credits against the current checkout instead of relying on expired claims.

Check validity

Confirm the coupon is visible in the current account or checkout, has not expired, and applies to the selected destination and route.

Compare the final total

A percentage discount may have a cap. Compare the parcel total after coupon, route fees, optional services and payment costs.

Avoid fixed promises

Promotions change. Treat old coupon codes, screenshots and referral claims as leads to verify, not guaranteed savings.

Coupon decision checklist

  1. Read the current terms.
  2. Check minimum spend and maximum discount.
  3. Confirm eligible routes and destinations.
  4. Compare the final payable amount.
