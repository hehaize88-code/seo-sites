Hipobuy spreadsheet and W2C links: find the right item

Use Hipobuy spreadsheets and W2C links to find products, verify variants and plan QC and shipping before ordering.

HipoBuys.de · Updated 2026-11-02

In this guide
  1. Verify a spreadsheet find

W2C means “where to cop”: a request for the source of a product. A Hipobuy spreadsheet or product index can help you discover a listing, but it does not establish stock, quality or the final delivered price. Open the current product page before making a decision.

Verify a spreadsheet find

Compare the item, seller, colour, size and quantity with the row you opened. Check that the image and destination match. A familiar photograph may represent several options with different prices. Record the actual variant and size chart, not only the spreadsheet title.

Use the product index to browse shoes, clothing or accessories, then return to the seller's current information. Avoid treating an old price or a community label as a guarantee. If a URL fails, the link troubleshooting guide explains how to distinguish an incomplete address from a missing or unsupported listing.

Plan the next steps before paying

Check seller-to-warehouse delivery and build a complete budget that includes the parcel stage. A cheap item can still create a bulky or otherwise unsuitable shipment. Confirm the route accepts the actual item type before selecting optional packing or combining goods.

After warehouse arrival, compare the QC photos with the exact selection you recorded. Ask focused questions while a correction is still possible. A spreadsheet is the beginning of research; the current listing, order record and warehouse evidence are the basis for approving the purchase and shipment.
