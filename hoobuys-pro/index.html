Buyer FAQ

What is a Hoobuy spreadsheet?

An independent spreadsheet records a current product route, exact option notes, QC status and parcel-planning data. Treat every row as a dated research record rather than a permanent product promise.

What should I check first in QC photos?

Begin with order identity: item type, colour, selected size and quantity. Then review overall shape, labels, measurements, included pieces and visible defects using a category-specific checklist.

Why can shipping estimates change?

Destination, item category, actual weight, volumetric weight, measured dimensions, available routes and selected parcel services all matter. The current warehouse record is a better basis than an old screenshot.
