How to make the decision

Start by confirming that the warehouse item is the option you selected. Then compare measurements and construction details that can actually change a keep-or-return decision. A useful photo request should answer one unresolved question; decorative angles rarely add value. Keep the order screenshot, selected option and QC images together so the evidence remains clear.

Warehouse images can show visible condition, colour, labels, measurements and obvious construction problems. They cannot prove long-term durability, exact material composition or performance that is not visible. If an important point remains uncertain, request a targeted measurement or close-up before submitting the parcel.
