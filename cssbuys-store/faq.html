CSSBuy Buying Essentials: Prices, QC and Delivery

Keep product selection, warehouse inspection and parcel delivery as separate decisions. These essentials explain where each check belongs.

Does the displayed product price include delivery?

Treat it as a dated product reference. Confirm the selected option and any domestic delivery or service charges at checkout. International delivery is a separate parcel cost.

What can warehouse QC photos verify?

They can show item identity, labels, visible condition, included parts and properly photographed measurements. They cannot guarantee comfort, authenticity or long-term durability.

Can I choose a size from the label alone?

Use the seller's measurement method and compare it with an item that fits. Keep foot length, insole length and outsole length separate; clothing width and circumference are also different measurements.

Why can the shipping estimate change?

Packing, verified weight, dimensions, route rules and charging increments can change the calculation. Save the inputs and recalculate when the final parcel information is available.

Does warehouse storage extend the return window?

No automatic extension should be assumed. Check the seller and order conditions, record the arrival date and review the available return action before its deadline.

Are fees identical for every purchase?

Check the current service and payment labels in the account. Record the calculation basis, fixed amounts and optional choices rather than copying a percentage from an old example.

Is this the official CSSBuy website?

No. This is an independent product-research and guide site. The product buttons open cnfanssp.com, a separate catalogue. Account, order and return questions belong with the relevant official service.

Where does the product search go?

It opens the matching search results on cnfanssp.com. Product cards open their corresponding listing, so you can verify the option and current price before continuing.
