CSSBuy FAQ: Shipping, QC, Returns and Storage

Practical answers for each stage of a CSSBuy order. Check current account terms before paying or shipping.

Is cssbuys.pro the official CSSBuy website?

No. This is an independent guide site. It does not accept orders, hold account balances, take warehouse photos or arrange shipping. Those services are handled through CSSBuy.

How do I order a product through CSSBuy?

Open the original product listing, check the seller and selected variation, then use the link in CSSBuy. Confirm size, colour, quantity and current charges before paying. If the link cannot be read, ask about the Expert Buy option.

Why are product payment and international shipping separate?

The item is purchased and delivered to the Chinese warehouse first. International shipping is arranged later, after warehouse checks and parcel preparation. Budget for both stages rather than treating the product price as the delivered price.

What should I include in a CSSBuy budget?

Record the product price, Chinese domestic delivery, any service and payment charges, currency conversion, optional warehouse services, international freight and applicable destination charges. Keep estimates separate from confirmed and paid amounts.

Does this site quote live CSSBuy shipping rates?

No. Our planning tools and examples explain weight and cost calculations. For available lines and current prices, use the official CSSBuy calculator with your destination, item attributes and packed measurements.

Why can two parcels with the same weight cost different amounts?

Their dimensions, contents, destination or eligible shipping lines may differ. A bulky parcel can have a higher chargeable weight. Compare complete quotes using the same inputs and check each line's billing rules.

How do I calculate volumetric weight?

Multiply the packed length, width and height using the units required by the line, then divide by that line's stated divisor. Check whether the route bills actual weight, volumetric weight or the larger amount, including any rounding.

How long does CSSBuy delivery take?

There is no single delivery time for every order. Seller dispatch, warehouse processing, packing, international transport and local delivery are separate stages. Use the current estimate for the selected line and allow for uncertainty.

How many free QC photos does CSSBuy advertise?

The official homepage reviewed on 4 November 2026 advertises 3–5 free QC photos. Confirm what is included for your specific order and check additional-photo or measurement options before requesting them.

Why can I see a warehouse weight but no QC photos?

A weight entry alone does not show whether the photo task is complete. Check the order status and selected services, then ask about the missing images in that order. Avoid approving shipment while an important QC question remains unanswered.

How much do extra photos or video checks cost?

Check the current service menu or ask CSSBuy for a quote describing the exact view or measurement you need. Do not assume additional photos, measurements or videos are free or available for every item.

Can QC photos prove authenticity or product performance?

No. Photos can help check the received variation, visible condition, measurements and included parts. They cannot establish every material, hidden defect, battery condition or long-term performance characteristic.

What should I do if the warehouse photos show the wrong size or damage?

Save the order details and relevant photos, describe the visible mismatch, and contact CSSBuy through the order promptly. Ask about return or replacement options before removing packaging or authorising international shipment.

How long do I have to request a warehouse return?

CSSBuy's public Q&A lists conditions including seller acceptance, less than seven days in the warehouse and payment of return postage. Check the actual order promptly; this is not an unconditional return promise for every product.

Is returning an item the same as withdrawing my account balance?

No. A product return concerns the seller and the item. A balance withdrawal concerns money already held in your CSSBuy account. Use the appropriate request and keep the two transaction records separate.

Will a refund automatically go back to my card or bank?

Do not assume it will. Check whether the transaction is an account credit or a refund to the original payment method. For a balance withdrawal, verify the available route, fees and processing information in your account or with support.

Why can the final shipping charge differ from the deposit?

The deposit uses estimated shipment information. Final verified dimensions, weight and line conditions can change the calculation. Compare the parcel invoice with the original estimate; check your account for any credit rather than assuming a bank refund.

How long is free warehouse storage?

Official product information distinguishes 90 days for regular goods and 30 days for sensitive goods. Check your item's classification and expiry date in the account; do not apply the general 90-day wording to every item.

Can I extend storage or reset it by combining items?

Ask about an extension before the displayed expiry date and confirm the price and affected order. Do not assume consolidation, a new purchase or a support enquiry resets the storage clock for older items.

What should I do if my stored item has expired?

Contact CSSBuy immediately with the order or parcel identifier and ask whether the goods are still available and what action remains possible. Do not assume an expired item is recoverable or that a past clearance notice gives you extra time.

Should I combine all my items into one parcel?

Compare a combined parcel with realistic split options. Check item compatibility, packed dimensions, fixed fees, protection and storage deadlines. A larger combined parcel is not automatically the least expensive suitable option.

Does removing shoe boxes always reduce shipping costs?

No. It can reduce volume, but the result depends on the final carton, billing rules and any packing fees. Consider the value of the box and protection needed for the shoes before requesting removal.

Is vacuum packing suitable for all clothing?

No. Review the garment's construction, material, shape and trims first. Compare simple folding, gentle compression and vacuum packing, and ask the warehouse to confirm what is suitable before applying pressure.

Can CSSBuy ship electronics with batteries or liquids?

Eligibility depends on the exact product, battery or liquid attributes, destination and current line rules. Provide accurate specifications and ask about an eligible route before placing the item into a mixed parcel.

What should I check when tracking stops updating?

Identify the last confirmed shipping stage and carrier, save the latest scan and time, and review the selected line's guidance. Use the parcel record for an enquiry; a gap in scans alone does not prove loss.

Does parcel insurance cover every loss, damage or customs problem?

Do not assume universal coverage. Check the current policy for your shipping line, including insured value, exclusions, reporting deadlines and evidence. Keep photos, invoices and packaging records that the policy requires.

How do I check a CSSBuy coupon, points reward or exchange rate?

Review eligibility, expiry, minimum spend, limits and where a reward can be used. Compare the final payment and credited balance separately from the advertised discount. A coupon does not establish the currency conversion rate.

What should I do when a product link no longer works?

Reopen the original seller listing and check the item identifier, stock and selected variation. Ask CSSBuy for help if parsing fails. Do not treat a different product reached through a replacement link as the same item without checking it.
