Spreadsheet Navigation Guide

A guide to spreadsheet organization and W2C path grouping.

A spreadsheet page should organize product routes by buyer intent, not just collect random links. W2C visitors need item type, QC difficulty, shipping sensitivity and whether the route still opens.

CSSBuy public guidance supports a link-first workflow: buyers can paste a product link from a Chinese ecommerce site or search by product name.

A good spreadsheet page can include columns for category, image, product path, QC reminder, shipping sensitivity and attribute caution.

The page should connect spreadsheet entries to QC articles. If a buyer finds a product in a spreadsheet, the next step is warehouse inspection and photo review.

Shipping notes also belong in the sheet. Shoes, bags, electronics, cosmetics, liquids and battery-related goods may require extra route checks.

Independent-site disclaimer: this site is an informational guide and does not operate CSSBuy, issue coupons, calculate exchange rates or process orders.

Build the sheet around decisions

A practical CSSBuy spreadsheet needs more than a product URL. Start with columns for the item name, category, source path, selected variation, seller price, domestic shipping and the date the listing was checked. Add warehouse columns only after the item arrives: warehouse item ID, received weight, stored dimensions if available, QC status and the deadline for any return decision. This keeps a product that looks interesting from being confused with one that has actually passed inspection.

Use controlled statuses such as Researching, Ordered, In warehouse, QC question, Approved, Return requested and Packed. Avoid free-form labels that mean the same thing in several ways. A filter should be able to show every item waiting for a QC decision or every bulky item that could increase volume weight. Color can help, but the written status must still work when the sheet is exported or viewed without formatting.

Add parcel planning without mixing costs

Create a separate parcel tab. Give each proposed parcel a temporary ID and list the included warehouse items. Record actual weight, estimated packaging, length, width, height, restricted attributes and the routes that remain available. Product cost belongs on the item tab; international shipping belongs on the parcel tab. If you want a per-item allocation for analysis, label the allocation method—by actual weight, volume share or equal split—so it is not mistaken for a carrier charge.

Keep a rate-check date beside every estimate. Save at least two scenarios: a compact parcel and a protected parcel with realistic packaging. For shoes, compare keeping and removing the box only when that choice is appropriate for the product. For fragile goods, do not lower the estimate by removing protection that the item needs.

Audit links before sharing the sheet

Open every public route in a clean browser session and check that it reaches the intended product rather than a search page, login wall or expired listing. Remove tracking parameters that are not needed, label unavailable products instead of silently replacing them, and never claim that a historical price is live. A useful public sheet includes a last-checked date and explains that product price, stock and shipping eligibility can change.

Finally, protect personal data. Order numbers, account balances, addresses, support screenshots and tracking identifiers do not belong in a public spreadsheet. Publish only the product and research fields that help another reader make an independent decision.

FAQ: CSSBuy facts, W2C and QC

How much does CSSBuy shipping cost?

CSSBuy shipping is route-based. Public examples include USA HZ-FEDEX-F at CN¥235 for the first 500g plus CN¥55 per additional 500g, Brazil FJ-BR-EXP at CN¥50 first 100g plus CN¥10.5 per next 100g, and Europe YW-CNLine-F at CN¥35 first 100g plus CN¥6.5 per next 100g.

Is shipping price fixed?

No. Final cost depends on destination, route, actual weight, parcel volume, product attributes and current line restrictions.

What does the CSSBuy calculator check?

It asks for product weight, volume, commodity attributes and destination, then estimates available shipping methods, transit time and shipping fee.

What is volumetric weight?

Volumetric weight is chargeable weight based on parcel dimensions. Many freight lines charge by whichever is larger: actual weight or volume weight.

How many free QC photos are provided?

CSSBuy public wording mentions unlimited QC images. I did not find a separate official per-item free-photo count in the reviewed pages.

How much is an extra QC photo?

I did not find a verified public extra-photo price. Users should check special image request pricing in their account or with official support.

Does CSSBuy publish a video QC price?

I did not find a public official video-QC or video-shooting price. These guides avoid inventing a fee.

What does warehouse QC check?

Public CSSBuy guidance describes warehouse inspection and photos for user review. Buyers should check appearance, size, shape, color, tags and obvious defects.

Can CSSBuy help with returns?

CSSBuy says returns can be helped when return conditions are met, the item has been in warehouse less than 7 days and return shipping fees are paid.

How long is free storage?

CSSBuy states warehouse storage is free for 90 days from the in-warehouse status.

What happens after 90 days?

Public Q&A states extension after 90 days costs 15 yuan per order per month.

What does W2C mean?

W2C means where to cop. It is the path from a keyword, image, spreadsheet entry or category route to a live product path.

How should W2C keywords be handled?

Use W2C guide pages for definitions and process; use category pages for item-type intent.

What does QC mean?

QC means quality check. In this context it refers to warehouse photos and review before international shipping.

Which QC keywords are useful?

Useful topics include QC photos, good QC, QC checker, how to check QC and category-specific QC checklists.

Can CSSBuy contact sellers?

CSSBuy describes Contact Seller and Search for me workflows for product inquiries and item-search help.

How fast are inquiry results?

CSSBuy says workers provide results within 1–2 days for Contact Seller or Search for me workflows.

Can users upload images?

CSSBuy says users can upload up to 3 images when describing what they are looking for.

Does CSSBuy publish exchange-rate markup?

I did not find a verified public exchange-rate markup percentage. Users should confirm live account and payment rates.

Are there coupons?

CSSBuy publicly mentions Review Rewards and points, but I did not verify a universal public coupon code.

What should a buyer check first?

Check item type, product route, QC expectations, weight, volume and restricted attributes before choosing shipping.

Is this an official CSSBuy website?

No. It is an independent informational site and does not process orders, payments, QC requests or shipping.

How should spreadsheets be organized?

By category, QC difficulty, shipping sensitivity and buyer intent rather than as a random link dump.

What should articles avoid?

Avoid fake coupons, invented video prices, invented exchange-rate markups and false official-affiliation claims.
