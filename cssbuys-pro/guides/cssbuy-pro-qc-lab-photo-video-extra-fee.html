CSSBuy Extra QC Photos: Missing Images, Requests and Fees

CSSBuys Pro Editorial · Updated 4 November 2026

If CSSBuy shows an item’s weight but no QC pictures, first check its warehouse status and the photo services attached to that specific order. A weight entry does not tell you whether the pictures are ready, whether you requested measurements, or whether an extra view has been accepted. Resolve those questions before adding the item to an international parcel.

This guide explains how to find missing images, request useful extra QC photos, check fees and decide whether to approve an item. CSSBuys Pro is an independent guide; photography and order support are handled through your CSSBuy account.

1. Only weight, no QC pictures: check three separate things

Start with the exact item ID, especially if several similar products arrived together. A seller’s delivered status, a warehouse record and a completed photo request describe different events. CSSBuy’s buying guide places inspection and photography after warehouse arrival. Do not treat a carrier’s delivery scan as confirmation that your image set is available.

  • Arrival and processing: read the item’s current status and recent notes. Ask whether warehouse intake or photography is still pending if the account does not make this clear.
  • Selected services: open the order details and check whether QC pictures, measurements or additional views were requested for this item.
  • Available results: open the item’s image area and any completed service entries. Inspect all thumbnails at full size; a measurement may be attached separately from the first overview picture.

If images remain missing, send one message identifying the item, the service selected and the result you cannot find. Attach a screenshot of the status and ask where the completed images are displayed or which step is outstanding. Missing pictures alone do not establish that the item is lost or damaged.

2. Standard photos, extra requests and fees

CSSBuy’s public homepage advertises three to five free QC photos. Treat that as a published service description, not a guarantee that every order includes every angle, measurement or video. Confirm the options attached to your item.

The help centre describes checking selected services in order details and adding options such as QC Pics or Measurements. Menu names and availability may differ in the current website or app; use the live order controls or ask support to locate them.

Before confirming an additional service, check the displayed total charge, currency and scope. Ask whether it covers one image, a set of views or a measurement, and whether a repeat request would be charged separately. If no price appears, request a quote before authorising paid work. This guide does not publish a universal extra-photo fee or a guaranteed turnaround time.

First review the pictures you already have. An extra close-up is useful when it answers an unresolved question that could change your decision, such as whether a mark is dirt or damage. Repeating the same overview without a different angle or clearer detail may leave that question unanswered.

3. Request measurements with exact endpoints

“Please measure it” leaves too much room for interpretation. Name the item, unit, start point, end point and position. Ask for the ruler’s zero mark and both endpoints to be visible in the same image, with the tape straight and the item laid naturally rather than stretched. Use the seller’s measurement method when comparing against a size chart.

QuestionSpecific photo request
Is the shirt wide enough?Lay it flat. Measure straight across from the left underarm seam to the right underarm seam, in centimetres, without stretching.
Is the garment too short?Measure from the highest shoulder point beside the collar to the bottom hem, in centimetres, with both endpoints visible.
What is the insole length?If the insole is removable without damage, measure from its rearmost heel edge to its foremost toe edge. Show the whole insole and tape in centimetres.
How wide is the bag?Measure its main body horizontally from the outer left edge to the outer right edge at the widest point, excluding handles and loose straps.

If a requested measurement is missing, refer to the original request and explain precisely what is absent. If the tape starts above zero or an endpoint is hidden, ask for a corrected view rather than estimating from the photograph. A flat garment width is not automatically the wearer’s chest circumference, and an insole measurement is not a guaranteed fit.

4. Fix unclear lighting and record the evidence

For a questionable stain, seam or label, request one overall view showing its location and one focused close-up. Ask for even, neutral lighting without a strong reflection. A different angle can help distinguish a shadow from a surface mark, but camera white balance and your screen can still alter colour. A photograph alone cannot establish the exact fabric composition.

Keep a short record containing the item ID, selected variation, original request, service charge if any, received images and unresolved question. For a mismatch, include the relevant seller listing or size-chart screenshot. Keep this evidence in the same order conversation so support can connect the request to the correct item.

A useful follow-up says: “The size label is readable, but the chest measurement is missing. Please show the tape from underarm seam to underarm seam.” It identifies the gap without asking the warehouse to guess what “better QC” means.

5. When video helps, and its limits

Ask whether a video service is available and what it costs before requesting it. Describe a short, observable action: show a zipper opening and closing from end to end, or a clasp fastening and releasing while the item remains visible. For a light or indicator, specify the condition you want shown and first confirm that staff can safely perform it.

A clip can document visible behaviour during that recording. It cannot prove full functionality, long-term durability, battery capacity or authenticity. CSSBuy’s inspection guidance describes a general check and notes limits on electronic testing. Do not interpret an illuminated screen as a complete technical inspection.

For electronics, liquids or other sensitive goods, review shipping restrictions before paying for extra checks. A clear video does not establish that a carrier will accept the item.

For example, suppose the received shirt has the correct size label but no chest measurement. That is an illustration of missing evidence, not proof that the size is wrong. Request the measurement first. If it then differs from the listing in a way you cannot accept, discuss a return with the documented comparison.

Keep an unresolved item out of a submitted parcel. Check its actual return deadline and warehouse storage deadline; requesting pictures should not be assumed to pause either one. The returns and refunds guide explains the next steps. Use the broader QC checklist to review the whole item.

7. Frequently asked questions

Why can I see weight but no QC photos?

Weight, warehouse processing and image availability are separate checks. Review the item’s status and selected services, then ask support which step remains incomplete.

Are all extra CSSBuy QC photos free?

Do not assume so. The public description of standard free photos does not specify every additional view. Check the live service price and scope for your order.

What if I paid for measurements but received only overview pictures?

Send the service record and original measurement request. Identify the missing dimension and ask where the result is attached or whether the request still needs completion.

How long do extra photos take?

Use the current order status or an item-specific support estimate. This guide cannot give one reliable completion time for all arrivals and service requests.

Can QC photos or video prove authenticity?

No. Visible details can inform your assessment, but warehouse images are not an authentication certificate or a complete functional test.
