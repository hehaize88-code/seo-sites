CSSBuy Extra QC Photos: Requests, Fees and Review Timing

CSSBuys Pro Editorial · Updated 7 October 2026

Plan a useful CSSBuy photo request, confirm service fees and review the received item before packing or shipping.

On this pageWrite a photo request that can be answeredQC is a decision gate

QC content helps buyers slow down before paying international shipping. A product route may be found, but warehouse photos decide whether the item is acceptable.

Review the current warehouse photo options for the received item. Confirm which views are available, what they show and whether additional images carry a charge before requesting them.

Before requesting extra photos or a video, check the current service menu or ask support to confirm availability and the total charge. Describe the evidence you need rather than paying for repeated views that do not resolve the issue.

A good QC checklist varies by item. Shoes need shape and sole checks. Hoodies need fabric, print and tag checks. Bags need hardware and lining checks. Electronics need package and surface-condition checks.

QC is connected to return timing. Check the current seller and service conditions for the specific item promptly, including the deadline, required evidence and any return delivery cost. Do not assume that one published deadline applies to every order.

Good QC does not mean perfection. It is a reasonable decision based on product type, price, defect risk and buyer tolerance.

Write a photo request that can be answered

“Take more photos” is vague. A useful request names the exact feature, angle and reference point: the outsole from directly above, the size tag with all text readable, the zipper closed and opened, or a ruler placed across a specific measurement. Before requesting anything extra, review every standard image and list the unanswered decision questions. This reduces duplicate requests and makes it easier to decide whether the response resolves the risk.

Use a simple evidence log with the request date, item ID, requested view, result and decision. If a service price appears only after entering the account workflow, record that live amount privately instead of turning it into a universal public fee. Video is similar: it is most useful when movement matters, such as a zipper, clasp or electronic indicator, but the request still needs a test condition and a visible outcome.

QC is a decision gate

Set three possible results: accept, request clarification or pursue return assistance. Do not let “more photos” become an automatic fourth stage with no decision rule. For each defect, record whether it is cosmetic, functional, size-related or a mismatch with the selected variation. Then compare the evidence with the return timing and current conditions shown in the account.

Warehouse photos cannot verify long-term durability, authenticity or every hidden component. State those limits plainly. The goal is to catch visible mismatches and obvious damage before international shipping, not to convert a photo set into a guarantee.

Before closing the QC record, note which limitations remain after the photos were reviewed. A clear limitation statement is more trustworthy than treating every unanswered question as a pass.
